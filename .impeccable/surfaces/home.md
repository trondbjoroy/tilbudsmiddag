# Surface brief: homepage and shared shell (index.html, recipe and topic pages)

Mode: Operate (plan the week's dinners, find the cheapest store). Audience: budget-tight households, mostly on a phone, at home or in the store aisle.
Keep: all functions, name, Norwegian copy, dish photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Prices are the product, so every price is a shelf-edge label. Refuses the warm cream + serif + terracotta food-blog default the site had.
OWN-WORLD: Cool shelf grey ground, label-white surfaces, ink-black type and actions, a dark shelf-rail header. Signal yellow appears only on offer price labels; the cheapest gets the yellow label. Rectangular, crisp corners (6px), hairline borders. Archivo (condensed widths) for headings, wordmark and price numerals; system sans for running text.
STORY: The visitor sees this week's dinners with their offer cost on a label, opens one, and gets a list that says which store has each item cheapest.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark rail with tag logo and nav (Ukemeny, Oppskrifter, Prissøk). Title, lead, week selector. Then the week plan: day heading, dish card with photo, name, offer items, and the yellow price label as the card's strongest element.
FORM: Hyllekant (shelf-edge price label), my first-ranked of seven candidates. seed: none (unavailable) — the concept-seed launcher was not run; the user picked from three self-derived directions plus the category standard.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
